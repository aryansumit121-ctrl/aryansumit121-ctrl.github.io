About

I am a PhD researcher in Library and Information Science at Mizoram University, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, semantic technologies, and digital libraries. My research focuses on computational approaches to knowledge organization, scholarly communication, citation analysis, and multilingual and Indigenous-language information access — with particular attention to research communities and knowledge systems that mainstream retrieval systems tend to underserve.

My work sits between two worlds: the technical (semantic search, embeddings, retrieval-augmented systems) and the institutional (how libraries, catalogues, and publishers actually organize and govern knowledge). I qualified UGC-NET with Junior Research Fellowship at the 99.908 percentile, and I hold a Master's in Library and Information Science from Banaras Hindu University.
